This Shiny app presents the results from a COVID-19 booster vaccine campaigns of Autumn 2023, Spring 2024, Autumn 2024, Spring 2025 and Autumn 2025.

The presented ShinyApp was developed supported by the Shiny package. On the one hand, this helps us assess the research-readiness of a set of cohorts we have defined from its data validity depiction. 
On the other hand, it prones evaluation ease within the different people involved in the study design, by displaying all the results from the study design. 

This assessment, when merged with rigorous manual modifications yields the exhibited ShinyApp. This includes:

- **Summary**  
  Collects the imported Results, providing not only the type of summary, but also its internal structure. 

- **OmopSketch**  
  Helps to better understand the database in which the cohorts were created. This includes descriptive features, such as its name and size, or operational characteristics, as could be the date or source of the extracted information.
- **Objective 1: Cohort Characterisation**  
  Provides information about the study population in each of the cohorts. It integrates the attrition for each cohort, which comprises the inclusion and exclusion criteria both for record and subjects determining if the later should be considered as part of the cohort.  

- **Objective 2: Coverage**  
  Displays booster vaccination coverage estimated across sociodemographic groups separately for each campaign of interest within eligibles.

- **Objective 3: Associated factors**  
  Within eligible individuals, factors associated with receipt of a booster vaccination are estimated independently for each campaign of interest while accounting for sociodemographic characteristics.
- **Logs**
  Illustrates the study workflow and the corresponding time allocated to each step.
